# Detailed-event backfill release

Index generated on 20 October 2026 at 04:53:38 UTC. Scope: season buckets 2022/23 onwards, with an inclusive match-date cutoff of 1 October 2026. Calendar leagues use the bucket starting in their calendar year. The latest eligible fixture is dated 30 September 2026; the cutoff does not imply every competition has fixtures or prices through that date.

## Outcome

- 36,605 unique fixtures pass the complete-event policy, across 29 of the 33 catalogued competitions.
- 15,291 additional indexed fixtures compared with the initial 21,314-match detailed-events-only release (`a800b16`). Every previously indexed match ID is retained, with its date, competition and score unchanged. Some display names are reconciled to the expanded provider aliases.
- 35,653 eligible fixtures have at least one usable market price; 952 are history-only until suitable odds are available. A particular market can have fewer priced fixtures.
- 49,577 unique fixtures are known to the combined local sources. The remaining 12,972 fail the full-event gate or have no event record. This denominator is not an independent inventory of every scheduled fixture.
- 120 filters pass the minimum of 100 distinct priced fixtures globally. Counts were independently recomputed from the final index; the smallest active sample is 106. Five filters remain retired. Narrower user scopes and combinations can still have smaller samples.

The full collection pass saved 15,579 validated event files. One interrupted Bundesliga request was retried once and recovered successfully, before the audit and index were rebuilt. These file counts are not additive to the initial website count: the initial release already included some early downloads, and the index reconciles provider duplicates. The earlier coverage pilot is also separate from the full-pass report.

All 165 competition/season combinations were checked. Seventeen detail responses (15 Serie B and two Brasileirao) did not match their fixture identities and were refused without relabelling. They were not treated as transient network failures or repeatedly retried. No access or rate-limit stop occurred. Incomplete responses were not promoted to valid event files. Collection remained single-request at the original rate.

## Remaining limitations

National League and the three Scottish lower divisions remain disabled: no records meeting the chosen detail standard were recovered. Older La Liga 2, Serie B, Ligue 2 and Greek seasons have substantial xG/shot-map gaps, with occasional qualifying records rather than complete seasons. Other competitions also have excluded fixtures, and UEFA qualifying rounds are not guaranteed complete. The in-site competition panel reports coverage by input and market.

Every indexed row must have both identified starting XIs and formations, core statistics including xG, a valid timed event timeline and a reconciled detailed shot map. Weather and player values remain optional, so not every pre-match filter input is available on every eligible match. Known missing/incomplete fixtures invalidate affected rolling windows and previous-match comparisons instead of letting them skip backwards. Fixtures absent from all sources cannot be detected by this safeguard; Elo still uses the unique eligible history.

Maximum bookmaker odds and the strictly pre-kickoff Betfair fallback policy are unchanged. Missing odds never produce returns. Named saved strategies keep their AND/OR groups; unavailable filter IDs block results until explicitly removed, and an unavailable saved competition cannot silently expand to all competitions. Results may change because the eligible match pool and historical inputs have improved.

Raw provider records, attempt caches and detailed job reports remain ignored under `data/sources/`. Only the derived index and this coverage summary are intended for publication on the existing GitHub Pages site.
